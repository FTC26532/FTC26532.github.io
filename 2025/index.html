FTC 2024–2025

2025 Season

The NH state FTC competition had over 30 teams competing — our team won Alliance Captain 2nd place, earning an invitation to the 2025 FTC New England Premier in West Springfield, Massachusetts.

The Competition

First Tech Challenge

Unlike FLL, FTC has 2 main judged categories — both focused on engineering excellence and robot performance.

🤖

1. Robot Game

Complete missions on the challenge board. Each year introduces a different theme and set of challenges to complete and gain points. This seems like the main focus — but it's only half of the picture.

🎤

2. Robot Design

A presentation to judges about how your robot works, the inspiration behind it, different iterations, and why it works well. You present each mechanism in detail.
