FTC 2024–2025

2025 Season

The New Hampshire state FTC competition had over 30 teams competing, and our team won alliance captain 2nd place! Since we acheived 2nd, we earned an invitation to the 2025 FTC New England Premier, in West Springfield, Massachusetts, in April.

The Competition

Competition Categories

Unlike FLL, FTC only contains 2 main judged categories, Robot Game and Robot Design.

01

Robot Game

Complete missions on the challenge board. This game seems like the main focus, but it isn't the whole competition. Each year, the Robot Game has a different theme and challenges to complete and gain points.

02

Robot Design

A presentation to tell judges about how your robot works, the inspiration, different interations, and why it works well. For Robot design, you will present to judges about your robot and how the different mechanisms work.
