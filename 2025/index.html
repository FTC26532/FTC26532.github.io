FTC 2024–2025

2025 Season

The NH state FTC competition had over 30 teams competing, and our team won Alliance Captain 2nd place — earning an invitation to the 2025 FTC New England Premier in West Springfield, Massachusetts.

The Competition

First Tech Challenge

Unlike FLL, FTC only contains 2 main judged categories — both focused on engineering excellence and robot performance.

🤖

1. Robot Game

Complete missions on the challenge board. This portion of the game seems like the main focus, but it isn't the whole competition. Each year, the Robot Game introduces a different theme and challenges to complete and gain points.

🎤

2. Robot Design

A presentation to tell judges about how your robot works, the inspiration, different iterations, and why it works well. For Robot Design, you present to judges about your robot and how the different mechanisms work.
