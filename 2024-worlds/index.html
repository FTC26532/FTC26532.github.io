FLL 2023–2024

2024 World Championship

NH State had 46 teams competing — our team got 1st place, earning an invitation to the 2024 FIRST Championship in Houston, April 17–20.

FLL Competition

Four Judged Categories

FLL is judged across four areas — and we excelled in all of them.

🤖

1. Robot Game

Complete missions on the challenge board. This seems like the main focus — but it's only part of the competition.

⚙️

2. Robot Design

Present to judges about how your robot works, the inspiration, different iterations, and why it works well.

💡

3. Innovation Project

Find and present a solution to a real-world problem based on the year's theme. Our project: HapLink.

🏆

4. Core Values

The most important category — teamwork and innovation. Our team got 100% in every Core Values category! (Discovery, Innovation, Impact, Inclusion, Teamwork, and Fun)
